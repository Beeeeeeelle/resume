# Belle Black Comet Visual Standard

Version: 1.0.0
Updated: 2026-08-31

## Identity Lock

Belle is a small human learner and researcher moving through ideas, evidence, and AI-mediated learning environments. She is not a generic cute girl, mascot sticker, anime character, or black silhouette figure.

The file `references/belle-canonical-current.jpg` is the primary identity reference. When a generated result conflicts with this guide or another pose image, the primary canonical image wins.

## Non-Negotiable Character Features

- Approximately 70% large swept black hair and 30% small body.
- Hair reads first as one continuous, near-solid black mass sweeping upward and backward.
- A few loose curved strands may break the outer edge, but the hair cannot become flames, spikes, tentacles, a detached cloud, or a long horizontal tail.
- Sparse warm-gold stars are embedded inside the black hair.
- Face is small and white with two tiny black vertical-dot eyes, a neutral mouth, and very light blush.
- No large eyes, highlights, eyelashes, exaggerated smile, or anime expression.
- Body is small and simple, with a black dress or black outfit and thin black arms and legs.
- One small four-wing butterfly hair clip sits on the visible upper hair mass.
- Hair clip layout is exactly 2 x 2 rounded wings with one black center dot:
  - upper left: dusty blue `#7B9CC8`
  - upper right: muted rose `#C8868E`
  - lower left: soft green `#82B896`
  - lower right: warm ochre `#D1A760`
- The clip is not a flower, bow, heart, star, or group of loose dots.

## Illustration Language

- Pure white background unless a supplied composition requires transparency.
- Fine, slightly irregular black hand-drawn lines.
- Large areas of white space.
- No paper texture, beige field, gradient background, heavy shadow, glossy 3D rendering, or polished commercial vector style.
- Main structural accents:
  - orange `#E05A2B` for paths and movement
  - blue for explanatory notes and boundary conditions
  - red only for a small number of critical problems or warnings
- Belle should perform a conceptual action rather than stand in a corner as decoration.

## Composition Rules

- Use one complete Belle in complex research figures.
- Represent repeated learners with abstract dots or compact head markers, not multiple complete Belle characters.
- Keep Belle visually secondary to the research structure in dissertation figures.
- Do not use a comet trail as the default connector. Use paths, notes, maps, rulers, arrows, tools, or evidence objects.

## Hard Rejection Conditions

Regenerate rather than repair when any of these occur:

- five- or six-petal hair clip, two clips, wrong colors, or floating clip
- ordinary long-haired girl, white dress, sticker mascot, or anime face
- large glossy eyes or a strong cute expression
- disconnected black hair cloud, gray pencil hair, dense white gaps, or fire-shaped hair
- multiple complete Belle characters crowding a research figure
- colored background, gradient, paper texture, dashboard, or generic presentation infographic
